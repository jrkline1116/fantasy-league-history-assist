Contact

Questions, bug reports, wrong stats, feature ideas and removal requests are all welcome.

How to reach us

Send a message on Buy Me a Coffee

You don't need to buy anything to send a message.

What to include

  • Your league's link (the address with # and a code at the end), so the right league can be found.
  • What you expected and what you saw. For a wrong stat, the season and week help a lot.
  • Your platform: Sleeper, public ESPN, or private ESPN.

Never send your ESPN cookies (espn_s2 or SWID). They aren't needed to look into a problem.

Removing a league

Send the league's link and say you'd like it removed. Saved league data is deleted once the request is confirmed. Anyone in the league can ask.

Before you write

  • Manager names can be changed by anyone with the link: Managers ✎ on the league page. Accounts can be merged there too.
  • Private ESPN league not updating? Any member can tap Update this season with their ESPN login. See private ESPN leagues.
  • Missing early Sleeper seasons? See how Sleeper history works.
