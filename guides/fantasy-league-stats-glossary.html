Fantasy League History Stats Glossary

Updated October 13, 2026

Every stat on a league history page, defined the way this site counts it. Where leagues disagree about how to count something (median wins, consolation games), the choice is spelled out.

Standings and finishes

Record (W-L-T)
Regular-season head-to-head wins, losses and ties. Sleeper's league-median wins aren't included, because they aren't games against an opponent.
Average finish
Your final place after the playoffs, averaged over finished seasons. When a platform doesn't report final places, playoff teams are ordered by how far they got (the 3rd-place game counts) and everyone else by regular-season rank.
Average regular-season finish
Your seed going into the playoffs, as the platform ranked it, averaged over seasons.
Titles
Championships won.
Playoff trips
Seasons you made the winners bracket.
Podium points
3 for a title, 2 for second, 1 for third. The all-time finishes list is ordered by podium points, then by better average finish, then by titles.

Points

Points for (PF)
Total points your starters scored in regular-season games. Shown with the per-game average in parentheses, like 1896.9 (145.9), so seasons of different lengths compare fairly.
Points against (PA)
Total points your opponents scored against you. A high PA per game usually means bad luck, not a bad team.
Points per game
PF divided by games played. The best single number for comparing teams across seasons.

Luck

All-play record
Your record if you'd played every other team every week. In a 10-team league, the week's top score goes 9-0. See all-play and luck, explained.
All-play %
Your all-play wins divided by all-play games (ties count as half).
Luck
Your actual win % minus your all-play %. Positive means you won more than your scoring earned.

Head-to-head

Head-to-head record
Every game between two managers: the regular season plus winners-bracket playoff games (playoffs can be toggled off). Consolation games are left out everywhere.
Beat most / lost to most
The opponent you have the most wins against, and the one with the most wins against you.
Streaks
Consecutive regular-season wins or losses. Streaks carry over from one season into the next.

Record book

Highest and lowest scores
Single-game team scores, any week.
Biggest blowout
Largest margin of victory in one game.
Closest game
Smallest margin of victory (ties aren't wins, so they aren't listed).
Highest-scoring season
Points per game over a full season, so 13- and 14-week seasons compare fairly.
Highest combined score
Both teams' points in one game added together.

Drafts, rosters and moves

Keeper
A player a manager kept from the year before instead of drafting fresh.
Still on roster at season's end
The share of a manager's draft picks who were on their final roster (finished seasons only). A rough measure of draft hits versus quick cuts.
Adds and drops
Completed waiver and free-agent moves. Failed waiver claims aren't counted, and players received in trades aren't adds.
Waiver claims
Adds that went through waivers, as opposed to free-agent pickups.
FAAB
Free Agent Acquisition Budget: a season-long budget managers bid from to win waiver claims. FAAB spent is the total of winning bids.
Can't quit him
The same manager drafting the same player in more than one season.

Who counts in superlatives

League bests like best average finish, best record, most points per game and luckiest manager only count managers who played at least half of the league's finished seasons. A manager who played one great year shouldn't top the all-time list.
