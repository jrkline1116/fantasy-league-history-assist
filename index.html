Questions

How do I see my fantasy league's history?

Enter your Sleeper username (or league ID), or paste your ESPN league's web address, and tap Load. Every past season loads automatically, back to the year your league started, and you get a link to share with the league.

Does it work with private ESPN leagues?

Yes. Either ask your commissioner to make the league viewable to the public (30 seconds in ESPN's League Manager tools), or use the one-click bookmark on a computer. Your ESPN login is used only to read the league and is never saved on our server.

Does my whole league need to sign up?

No. One person loads the league and shares the link. Everyone else just opens it on any phone or computer, no account or login needed.

What stats does it show?

All-time standings and record, average finish, titles, playoff trips, points for and against (with per-game averages), head-to-head records against every manager, all-play record and luck, the league record book, every draft, final rosters, trades and waiver pickups.

A leaguemate changed accounts. Can I combine them?

Yes. Open Managers, then merge the old account into the new one. Their records combine, and the change shows for everyone with the link (and can be undone).

Is it free?

Yes, free for Sleeper and ESPN leagues. It isn't affiliated with ESPN or Sleeper.
